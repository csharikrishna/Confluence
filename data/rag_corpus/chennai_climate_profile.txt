Document Reference: IMD-RMC-CHN-CLIM-2023
Published Date: 2023-04-15
Authority: India Meteorological Department, Ministry of Earth Sciences, Government of India
Location: Chennai Coastal Zone (Nungambakkam & Meenambakkam Observatories, 13.08° N, 80.27° E)

1. CLIMATOLOGICAL SUMMARY & SEASONAL NORMALS
Chennai experiences a tropical wet and dry climate with high relative humidity throughout the year (average 65% to 80%). 
- Winter (January–February): Mean maximum temperature 29.3°C, mean minimum 20.8°C. Relative humidity averages 72%. Winds are predominantly northeasterly at 8–12 km/h. Sea state is typically slight with average significant wave heights between 0.6m and 0.9m.
- Summer (March–May): Mean maximum temperature rises to 37.4°C in May, with extreme historical peaks reaching 42.8°C. High humidity produces apparent temperatures (heat index) regularly exceeding 40.0°C. Winds are southeasterly at 12–16 km/h.
- Southwest Monsoon (June–September): Chennai lies in the rain shadow of the Western Ghats; rainfall during this period averages 440 mm total. Maximum temperatures remain elevated at 35.0°C to 36.5°C.
- Northeast Monsoon (October–December): Principal rainfall season providing ~800 mm (60% of annual rainfall). November is the wettest month with normal precipitation of 375 mm. Active cyclonic disturbances in the Bay of Bengal produce sustained wind speeds of 45–65 km/h and wave swells exceeding 2.5m.

2. HISTORICAL EXTREMES & RECORDED BASELINES
- Highest Recorded Maximum Temperature: 45.0°C (May 31, 2003)
- Lowest Recorded Minimum Temperature: 13.9°C (December 11, 1895)
- Heaviest 24-hour Rainfall: 452.4 mm (November 25, 1976); 345.0 mm (December 1, 2015)
- Mean Barometric Pressure: 1008.5 hPa in January; 1002.1 hPa in June; cyclonic depressions regularly drop below 995.0 hPa.

3. ARTISANAL FISHERIES ADVISORY BASELINES
Traditional kattumarams and motorized fiberglass boats (FRP) operate safely when wave heights remain below 1.5 meters and wind speeds are under 15 knots (28 km/h). During non-monsoon months (February to April), morning sea conditions along Marina and Besant Nagar are generally favorable for nearshore artisanal gillnetting within 5 nautical miles.
